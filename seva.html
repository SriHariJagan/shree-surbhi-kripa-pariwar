Our Seva

Service with hands, heart, and reverence.

Four expressions of one devotion — Gau Seva, Sanskriti, Samaj, and Arogya.

The Work

The work is simple. The meaning is infinite.

We do not need complicated language to explain what we do. We feed. We care. We protect. We gather. We teach. We serve. And in doing so, we discover that we receive far more than we give.

Gau Seva

Daily care for Gau Mata.

Every morning begins with fodder and fresh water. Through the day, our team and volunteers tend to the needs of cows and calves — clean shelter, grooming, gentle handling, and medical support when required. For injured or vulnerable animals, care is slower, closer, and more attentive.

  • Fresh green and dry fodder
  • Clean drinking water
  • Shelter cleaning and bedding
  • Grooming and wound care support
  • Veterinary care coordination
  • Loving, patient handling
Support daily Gau Seva

Sanskriti

Keeping the sacred alive.

Havan, katha, bhajan, and satsang are not separate from seva; they are the breath of it. Our monthly gatherings bring devotees together for prayer, reflection, and community. Young people are invited not as spectators but as participants — learning mantra, discipline, and respect through lived experience.

  • Monthly havan and satsang
  • Katha and pravachan
  • Festival celebrations
  • Youth sanskar and temple engagement
  • Guided reflection and spiritual companionship
Join a satsang

Samaj

Service that reaches beyond the gaushala.

Gau Seva is connected to the health of our neighbourhoods, our youth, and our shared spaces. We participate in cleanliness drives, community gatherings, and support initiatives that strengthen the local fabric. When we care for the earth and for one another, we care for Gau Mata too.

  • Cleanliness and shramdaan drives
  • Community gatherings and celebrations
  • Youth engagement and mentorship
  • Support for vulnerable families, as capacity allows
  • Awareness on cow care and local ecology
Serve the community

Arogya

Wellness rooted in tradition.

Through the spirit of Kamdhenu Arogya Sansthan, we encourage wellness practices inspired by Ayurveda and traditional care — always alongside, never instead of, qualified medical advice. Our focus is gentle: better daily habits, greater awareness, and compassionate support.

  • Ayurveda-inspired wellness awareness
  • Traditional food and lifestyle guidance
  • Community health conversations
  • Referral to qualified practitioners where needed

How Your Support Helps

Three ways to give.

Time

Feeding, cleaning, grooming, and simply sitting with the cows.

Resources

Fodder, water, medicines, shelter upkeep, and veterinary care.

Skills

Design, photography, accounting, veterinary knowledge, teaching, outreach.

Come for a morning, stay for a lifetime. There is work for every pair of hands and warmth for every heart.
