Why Give

A small act. A living difference.

Gau Mata gives us so much — nourishment, agriculture, companionship, and a living connection to our culture. Our seva is a chance to give back. Your donation supports daily fodder and water, shelter upkeep, veterinary care, and community initiatives. It is not a transaction. It is a relationship.

No amount is too small, and no sevak is too new. Give only what feels right to you.

Good to Know

Donation questions.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. You pay directly through your own UPI app or bank. We never ask for your card number, UPI PIN, OTP, or banking password. Never share these with anyone, including someone claiming to represent us.

Which apps can I use?

Any UPI-enabled app, including GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and most bank apps.

Why does the payee name look different?

The registered UPI name is “MSSHREESURBHIKRIPAPARIWAR”. Your app may display it in capital letters. The UPI ID ends with @icici.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes, on request. Email your name, amount, date, and UTR to shreesurbhikripapariwar@gmail.com.

Can I donate from outside India?

UPI requires an Indian bank account. For international transfers, please write to us first so we can guide you correctly.

Can I visit the gaushala?

Yes, by appointment. Please message us before visiting so we can welcome you properly.

Is my donation refundable?

Donations are generally non-refundable. If you have made an error, write to us within seven days and we will review it in good faith.

Our promise to you.

  • We will use your contribution for Gau Seva and the initiatives described on this website.
  • We will not sell or share your personal information.
  • We will issue a receipt on request.
  • We will correct any mistake promptly.
  • We will never pressure you to give. Give only what feels right.
