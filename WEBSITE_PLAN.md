## 1. Project Summary

### 1.1 One-line positioning

**A premium, devotional home for Shree Surbhi Kripa Pariwar — where Gau Seva, Sanatan Sanskriti and community come together.**

### 1.2 Primary goal

Turn warm interest into **trust and participation**: donations, volunteers, gaushala visits, and invitations for Acharya Ji.

To serve Gau Mata with daily love and care, keep Sanatan Sanskriti alive through satsang and sanskar, and bring people together in selfless service.

### 2.2 Vision

A community where every cow is cared for, every tradition is carried forward with understanding, and every person has a way to serve.

### 2.3 Values

| Value | What it means on the website |
|---|---|
| **Seva** | Service before self; practical, daily, unglamorous work |

| **Sanskriti** | Respect for tradition, without superstition or aggression |
| **Satya** | Honest storytelling; no invented numbers or claims |
| **Sahaj** | Warm, simple, welcoming; no pressure or fear-based appeals |

**25 words:**  
Shree Surbhi Kripa Pariwar is a devotee-led family in Gurugram, serving Gau Mata daily and keeping Sanatan traditions alive through seva, satsang and community care.

Inspired by GauPremi Acharya Manish Ji, Shree Surbhi Kripa Pariwar brings devotees together for daily Gau Seva, monthly havan and satsang, youth sanskar, and community initiatives in Gurugram. It is a simple invitation: come as you are, and serve with love.

**100 words:**  
Shree Surbhi Kripa Pariwar is a living family of devotees rooted in Gurugram, Haryana. Guided by GauPremi Acharya Manish Ji, the Pariwar serves Gau Mata through daily fodder, water, shelter and care; nurtures Sanatan Sanskriti through havan, katha and satsang; and strengthens the community through youth engagement, cleanliness drives and neighbourly support. ### 2.6 Tone of voice

- **Warm, not salesy.** Speak like a host welcoming a guest.
- **Devotional, not dramatic.** Use "Gau Mata" naturally, not as a slogan.
- **Honest, not inflated.** Say "support", "help", "care" — only use numbers that can be proven.
- **Inclusive, not political.** No party politics, no communal aggression, no implied endorsements.
- **Bilingual by instinct.** Hindi/Sanskrit lines carry the emotion; English carries the explanation.

### 2.7 Naming rules

| Correct | Avoid |

**Note:** The name "Acharya Manish" is shared by other public figures. Always qualify as **GauPremi Acharya Manish Ji** and connect him to Shree Surbhi Kripa Pariwar to avoid confusion.

## 3. Audience & Journeys

### 3.1 Primary audiences

| Audience | What they need | What we show them |
|---|---|---|
| **Devotees & families** | Darshan, satsang, a way to serve | Acharya Ji, events, gallery, donate |
| **Donors** | Trust, clarity, easy payment | QR, UPI, bank details, receipt process |
| **Volunteers** | A real way to help | Seva options, form, WhatsApp |
| **Event organisers** | To invite Acharya Ji | Invite page, event types, form |
| **Local community** | Proof of genuine work | Photos, press, location, contact |
| **Media** | Background, quotes, images | Press page, boilerplate, contact |
| **NRI / out-of-town supporters** | Remote giving | UPI note, bank transfer, international caveat |

### 3.2 Primary donor journey

1. Lands on Home from Instagram, WhatsApp, or search.
2. Feels the mission through the hero and Gau Seva imagery.
3. Sees a real face, a real place, and real work.
4. Clicks **Donate** (header, hero, donation band, or sticky mobile bar).
5. Scans the QR or copies the UPI ID / bank details.
6. Pays in their own UPI app.
7. Clicks **Share payment confirmation** → WhatsApp/email with name, amount, UTR.
8. Receives a thank-you and receipt from the team.
9. Optionally joins the WhatsApp/Instagram community.

### 3.3 Volunteer journey

1. Clicks **Become a Sevak**.
2. Chooses a seva: gaushala care, events, media, medical, teaching, outreach.
3. Submits name, city, phone, availability.
4. Receives a WhatsApp message with the next seva opportunity.
5. Attends, serves, and is invited back.

### 3.4 Key friction points to remove

- "Is this genuine?" → real photos, press, named guide, location, direct contact.
- "How do I pay?" → QR first, UPI ID copy, bank transfer, mobile deep link.
- "Will my money be used well?" → specific seva categories, receipt process, no vague promises.
- "I don't have money." → volunteer, share, donate fodder, join satsang.
- "Is this political?" → neutral language and captions.

## 5. Design Direction

### 5.1 Creative concept: **The Golden Gaushala**

*(A design concept, not a claim about physical facilities.)*

A quiet, premium devotional world — not a crowded temple poster. Think warm cream paper, deep maroon, saffron and antique gold; the calm of an early-morning gaushala; the intimacy of a handwritten invitation; the confidence of an editorial magazine.

**Three words:** Sacred. Warm. Unhurried.

**Reference mood (quality bar, not imitation):** Indian heritage editorial design, luxury Ayurveda packaging, museum exhibition catalogues, hand-block textile patterns, brass temple lamps, fresh marigold garlands.

### 5.2 Colour palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--maroon-900` | `#2B0808` | Hero background, footer, dark bands |
| `--maroon-800` | `#4A0E0E` | Section backgrounds |
| `--saffron-600` | `#D96A1A` | Primary buttons, links |
| `--saffron-500` | `#E8751A` | Accents, icons |
| `--marigold-400` | `#F5A623` | Highlights, gradients |
| `--gold-500` | `#C9A227` | Borders, small ornaments |
| `--gold-300` | `#E4C978` | Foil lines, hover glow |
| `--cream-50` | `#FDF9F1` | Page background |
| `--cream-100` | `#FBF3E4` | Cards, alternate sections |
| `--sand-200` | `#EFE1C8` | Dividers, subtle fills |
| `--green-700` | `#1E4D2B` | Nature / Gau Seva accent |
| `--ink-900` | `#211A17` | Body text |
| `--ink-600` | `#5E514A` | Secondary text |

**Rules:** Gold is for lines, icons and large display type only — never small body text on cream. Body text is maroon/ink on cream for contrast. Use the green sparingly, as a leaf among marigolds.

### 5.3 Typography

| Role | Font | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display Latin | **Cormorant Garamond** | 400, 500, 600, 700 | Elegant, editorial, slightly sacred |
| Display Devanagari | **Tiro Devanagari Hindi** | 400 | Authentic, calligraphic feel |
| Body / UI | **Mukta** | 300, 400, 500, 600, 700 | Excellent Devanagari + Latin, warm and readable |

**Scale**

| Element | Size | Line height |
|---|---|---|
| Hero H1 | `clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)` | 0.95–1.05 |
| Section H2 | `clamp(2.2rem, 4.2vw, 4rem)` | 1.05 |
| H3 | `1.35–1.75rem` | 1.2 |
| Body | `17–19px` | 1.7 |
| Eyebrow | `12–13px`, uppercase, `0.18em` tracking | 1.4 |
| Quote | `clamp(1.6rem, 3vw, 2.8rem)` | 1.3 |

**Details:** Use Devanagari for emotional headlines and shloks; English for explanation and navigation. Never letterspace Devanagari. Use real hanging punctuation and generous margins.

### 5.4 Layout & grid

- Max content width: **1240px**; text measure: **62–72ch**.
- 12-column desktop grid, 8-column tablet, 4-column mobile.
- Section padding: `clamp(72px, 10vw, 140px)` vertical.
- Asymmetry is welcome: 5/7 or 7/5 splits, not everything centred.
- Use **temple arch** masks for portraits and feature images.
- Generous whitespace; let the photography breathe.

### 5.5 Ornament & texture

- Hand-drawn **temple arch** SVG linework.
- Subtle **mandala** line pattern at 3–5% opacity.
- **Marigold** petal accents, used sparingly.
- **Om** watermark behind the hero or donation band.
- **Brass diya** flicker (CSS, reduced-motion safe).
- Fine **paper grain** overlay on cream sections (very subtle).
- Gold **foil rules** between major sections, not around every card.
- Cow and calf silhouette used as a small brand mark, never as a cartoon.

### 5.6 Imagery direction

**Do**

- Crop portraits to 4:5 or 1:1 with the face on a third.
- Use warm, natural colour grading — golden highlights, soft shadows.
- Keep the cow's eye and human hands in focus; these are the emotional anchors.
- Pair a wide event shot with a close portrait for rhythm.
- Use the river portrait as the signature hero image.
- Add a fine grain to unify WhatsApp-compressed photos.

**Don't**

- Don't upscale the smallest images (468×564, 419×601, 403×1339).
- Don't use newspaper scans as background texture; they are unreadable and copyrighted.
- Don't over-saturate saffron until skin tones look orange.
- Don't use photos of public figures to imply endorsement.
- Don't put text over busy faces.

### 5.7 Motion language

| Moment | Motion |
|---|---|
| Page load | Hero text rises 24px, fades in over 0.8s; image unmasks from an arch |
| Scroll | Sections reveal with 0.6–0.8s ease; images scale 1.03 → 1 |
| Hero | Very slow Ken Burns (20s) on the river image |
| Marquee | Gentle 30s loop of values/shloks |
| Buttons | Gold shimmer sweep on hover; 2px lift |
| Cards | Image zoom 1.04, caption underline draws in |
| Counters | Count up once when in view |
| Gallery | Lightbox with keyboard, swipe, and caption |
| Donate | QR card glows softly; copy button shows "Copied" |
| Reduced motion | All transforms disabled; opacity-only fades |

**Avoid:** autoplay music, pop-ups, fake countdowns, excessive cursor followers, scroll-jacking, confetti.

### 5.8 Component language

**Buttons**

- Primary: saffron-to-marigold gradient, dark maroon text, 999px radius, 14px 24px padding, soft shadow.
- Secondary: transparent with 1px gold border, cream text on dark / maroon text on light.
- Tertiary: text link with animated underline.

**Cards**

- 24px radius, cream surface, 1px `rgba(201,162,39,.25)` border, soft warm shadow.
- No heavy drop shadows; use light and border.

**Navigation**

- Transparent over hero, then cream/blur with maroon text on scroll.
- Active link marked by a small gold dot, not a heavy underline.

**Donation card**

- Deep maroon surface, gold arch frame, white QR tile, UPI ID in monospace, copy button.
- Bank details in a collapsible panel to reduce clutter.

### 8.1 Principles

1. **Trust first.** Show the name, bank, and UPI clearly. Never hide fees or use a personal number without explanation.

5. **Honest tax language.** No 80G claim until a valid certificate is on file.
